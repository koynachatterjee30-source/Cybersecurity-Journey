# Email & Username Reconnaissance

Email and Username Reconnaissance is the process of identifying publicly available email addresses, usernames, and related digital information during cybersecurity reconnaissance.

This type of reconnaissance is mainly used to understand an organization's or individual's publicly exposed digital footprint and to identify information that may create security risks.

## Why Email & Username Reconnaissance is Important

Publicly exposed email addresses and usernames can provide useful information during a security assessment.

They may help identify:

- Public email addresses
- Employee usernames
- Organization accounts
- Social media profiles
- Public GitHub accounts
- Publicly available documents
- Possible naming conventions
- Digital footprints

Security teams can use this information to understand their exposure to phishing, impersonation, and social-engineering attacks.

## Email Reconnaissance

Email reconnaissance involves finding email addresses that are already publicly available.

Possible sources include:

- Official company websites
- Contact pages
- Public documents
- Search engines
- Public GitHub repositories
- Professional networking profiles
- Public conference pages
- Company directories

Only publicly available information should be collected during passive reconnaissance.
